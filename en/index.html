An unusual textbook on jewellery-business marketing — an interactive one. It was written by two professionals: Professor of gemmology Nona Dronova and successful entrepreneur Sergey Gribnyakov, founder of the SUNLIGHT holding. Every idea has been tested in practice — from the first deals of the 1990s to Russia’s largest jewellery chain, marketplace and media holding.

The SUNLIGHT Holding

The SUNLIGHT holding brings together the SUNLIGHT jewellery chain and marketplace, the SUNLIGHT pawnshop network, the Academy of Diamonds and Moscow Chain Factory brands, SERGEY GRIBNYAKOV watches and the “Dating” app. Their experience is the material of this textbook.
