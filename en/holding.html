The SUNLIGHT Holding

The SUNLIGHT holding brings together the SUNLIGHT jewellery chain and marketplace, the SUNLIGHT pawnshop network, the Academy of Diamonds and Moscow Chain Factory brands, SERGEY GRIBNYAKOV watches and the “Dating” app. It was built by Sergey Gribnyakov, and its experience is the basis of this textbook.

SUNLIGHT

Russia’s largest chain of jewellery stores and a modern marketplace for jewellery and watches: a wide choice from hundreds of brands, round-the-clock advice and free delivery. SUNLIGHT keeps renewing its brand philosophy, stores and collections, and uses Yakutian diamonds in its jewellery.

The marketplace also sells partner brands such as Kruger studios and BASTET silver. SUNLIGHT was the first retailer to launch its own video platform, SUNLIGHT Play, inside its mobile app. SUNLIGHT even has its own anthem, “A Dream of a Diamond”.

Sonic branding

SUNLIGHT anthem “A Dream of a Diamond”

An own song is a rare brand tool in retail. Music is remembered faster than a logo and creates an emotion people then recognise in ads, in the store and in the app.

SUNLIGHT Pawnshops

A network of pawnshops under the holding’s shared brand: jewellery valuation, secured loans, gold buy-back and exchange. The pawnshop, buy-back and exchange sit in the SUNLIGHT website’s main menu next to the stores — customers see that jewellery can be not only bought but also valued, exchanged or sold back.

Academy of Diamonds

A premium diamond brand. Its flagship store is on Kuznetsky Most in central Moscow, with further stores in major shopping centres. The brand has its own identity: orange colour, a laurel crest with the “AB” monogram, branded packaging and tags, and its own mobile app.

Product video card

Academy of Diamonds: from rough diamond to jewellery

In 24 seconds the clip takes the customer along the stone’s whole path: natural rough diamond, cutting, the jeweller’s work, the finished piece. Each step names its standard (GOST, TU, OST), and it ends with a lifetime guarantee. A short video turns trust into an argument: not “take our word for it” but “here is the standard, here is the guarantee”.

The brand runs the Scientific Gemmological Centre — a laboratory for identifying, classifying and certifying stones, with Professor Nona Dronova as Scientific Director.

Moscow Chain Factory

A gold-chain brand: a wide assortment, manufacturer prices and 30 years of history. Its identity is built on a constructivist “МЦЗ” monogram and Moscow motifs.

SERGEY GRIBNYAKOV watches

The holding founder’s own brand. The watches are sold through SUNLIGHT under the slogan “Worthy of your story”; the faceted clover-stone emblem appears on the dial and is engraved on the caseback.

Dating

The dating section of the SUNLIGHT mobile app: users upload videos and earn SUNLIGHT bonus roubles — an example of a jewellery retailer turning its store app into a social platform.

The SUNLIGHT ecosystem

The SUNLIGHT website footer shows the whole holding: stores and catalogue, pawnshop, gold buy-back and exchange, loyalty programme and gift card, pay-in-instalments, a “Brand Encyclopaedia”, “All about diamonds”, “About the jewellery trade”, an e-library, SUNLIGHT Dating, apps for iOS, Android, Huawei and RuStore, and its own media — SUN Magazine (“On beauty and life”) and DICH’ (“Media for men”). A good textbook case: a jewellery brand building a whole environment around the purchase — services, content and community.
