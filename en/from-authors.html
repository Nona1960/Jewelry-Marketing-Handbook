From the authors

You came into this world to live in success. Success is well-being in every area of life: health, a happy family, good friends, work that matches your true calling, enough money and time to carry out all the right and important decisions and to rest properly, freedom, pleasant memories, a rich store of inner experience, openness and honesty towards yourself and others, loyalty, and the ability to see the truth through illusion.

Real well-being includes the desire for success — not for the sake of success itself, but for the satisfaction it brings. Success cannot be conquered. It can be achieved, and only where you take pleasure in what you do.

The strategic ideal of success in the jewellery market is highly rational, emotionally balanced action aimed at winning with minimal losses.

Why do you need success in the jewellery market? Lasting success:

  • helps you set the prices that suit you, or win a larger share of the market while keeping prices at the same level;
  • gives your products a clear standing and removes the barriers to new sales territories;
  • gives you a solid base for launching new products;
  • increases customer loyalty;
  • raises staff morale and makes people proud of the company;
  • helps you hire the best people;
  • makes your firm more attractive to potential business partners.

Success is built over a whole lifetime, depends on many different factors and has many components. A well-crafted image helps a great deal. Image is what you do, how you feel and behave, and what people actually see in you: your appearance, your personal history, your style of dress, your reputation. If we do not think about our outward image, it forms without our will and is shaped largely by how other people perceive us.

In the jewellery business it is very important to create the image of an exceptionally successful person, because business reputation has above all an economic meaning. Your partners and customers want to see in you a reliable and self-confident leader.

Building your image consciously lets you manage the impression you make on others, influence them, and become an active participant in your relationships.

You will succeed if you follow three main rules:

  1. Always remember and take care of your image.
  2. Be confident (if you are not confident in yourself, your customers and partners will certainly have no confidence in you).
  3. Position your image correctly in relation to your competitors.

You must always be an individual.

Nona Dronova, Sergey Gribnyakov
