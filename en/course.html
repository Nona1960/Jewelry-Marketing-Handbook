The art of selling gemstone jewellery

Gemmology professor Nona Dronova has worked with precious stones for forty years: at the jeweller’s bench, in the laboratory and in creating jewellery brands. In this course she teaches the key skill of a sales consultant — talking about a stone so that the customer sees its value and wants to wear it.

Lesson 5

Emerald: beautiful words that attract the customer

The video is in Russian; the lesson’s words are translated below.

9 words about emerald

  1. 1Exquisite
  2. 2Magnificent
  3. 3Vivid colour
  4. 4Natural (of natural origin)
  5. 5Rare
  6. 6Very clear
  7. 7Alive
  8. 8Genuine, natural
  9. 9Looks expensive

Phrases for selling emerald

  • A timeless value
  • A good investment
  • A stone that brings good luck
  • A symbol of wealth
  • Exquisite and unique
  • A true work of art and nature
  • A stone that never goes out of fashion

How to use the lesson’s words

  • One or two words, not all at once. Customers remember an image, not a list. Pick what is visible in this particular stone: colour, clarity, play of light.
  • Word plus proof. “See how alive the colour is in daylight” is stronger than just “alive”. Show the stone by the window, through a loupe, on the customer’s hand.
  • The sales phrase comes last. “A timeless value” or “a good investment” is convincing once the customer has fallen for the stone, not in the first minute.
  • Only the truth about the stone. “Natural” and “genuine” describe natural emeralds only. From 1 September 2026 these words may not be used for synthetic stones in Russia (see chapter 11). Honesty in describing a stone is the basis of trust and repeat purchases.

Practical task

Take three emeralds or emerald pieces from your display. For each, choose two words from the lesson and one sales phrase that truly fit it. Say them aloud to a colleague while showing the stone, and ask: would you believe it as a customer?
