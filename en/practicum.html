Own channel or marketplace: which pays better?

Marketplace—profit per customer over lifetime

How it is calculated

  • Own channel: LTV = order × margin × purchases per year × years − acquisition cost.
  • Marketplace: acquisition is “free”, but every purchase pays a fee, and repeat purchases often go to other sellers on the same platform. The first purchase counts in full, repeats in the share you enter.
  • Compare the results and think about why SUNLIGHT keeps customers in its own app while a marketplace can be a launch pad for a small brand (see chapter 10).
