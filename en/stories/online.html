Online sales are the present and the future

In this chapter

  1. How is a marketplace different from an online store?
  2. A good website and an ordinary one: what is the difference?

Buying jewellery online is becoming as routine for us as buying everyday goods. On the way home from university or work, or while waiting for your child at the swimming pool, you can choose and order gifts for yourself and your loved ones: new earrings for your mother, a fashionable chunky bracelet for yourself, a silver spoon for a goddaughter, a tie clip or a watch for your father or husband. Online shopping saves the buyer time and sometimes money, and helps the seller succeed commercially. Sergey Gribnyakov, owner of SUNLIGHT, explains why a jewellery chain cannot grow today without online sales.

– Is there anything special about promoting an online trading platform?

– Absolutely. We use national communication channels to advertise our online store sunlight.net. It is one of the largest in Russia and in the world by sales volume, assortment, number of suppliers, service and many other measures. News about the online store that interests internet users, up-to-date information that matters to everyone — this is a great incentive to download the app (SUNLIGHT on Google Play) right after reading the news and find yourself, virtually, on the sales floor that very minute: see how the online jewellery hypermarket works and what it offers, look at photos and video reviews of jewellery and read customer reviews.

Of course you can advertise products, promote news hooks and keep up the frequency of brand mentions — all of that helps. But when the PR team produces news about online trading, the assortment, competitive advantages, the brand app and its reviews, it bears quite different fruit, because you can immediately see leads and click-throughs — the reaction and interest of potential buyers. That is what we want to advertise and promote.

– What are the main advantages of selling jewellery online?

– A company that sells online is worth much more than a retail chain, because online sales operate on a completely different scale. Say that to grow a retail business you need to open another 500 stores; to increase the capacity of an online store you need nothing: if there are fifty times more orders, we can ship fifty times more. From the point of view of maths, advertising and PR, the online channel has a great many opportunities for growth.

How is a marketplace different from an online store?

A marketplace has no goods of its own: people list their goods on the platform and sell them themselves — Wildberries or Ozon, for example. SUNLIGHT sells its own goods, and that is called online retail. This online retail has two routes: one to the customer’s door, the other to a store, where the customer comes to the nearest shop and collects the order.

– Do you read the jewellery reviews on the website or in the app yourself?

– Of course.

– What in this feedback pleases or upsets you and your team the most?

– We track and collect all negative reviews; they scroll continuously on a screen in the open-plan office. You read them and your heart bleeds: this isn’t right, that isn’t right, the piece isn’t what they wanted… Even so, it is a small share compared with the reviews from satisfied customers. The ratings under every piece on the website, like in Yandex.Taxi, are genuine, and we publish negative reviews as well as positive ones. If a piece has a rating of 4.5, 4.8 or 5, that is the overall level of customer satisfaction; it is not moderated in any way.

– How do you attract and hold the interest of people who have never bought from the SUNLIGHT online store, so that they become regular customers?

– Affordable prices, high quality, a wide assortment, promotions — those are the classics. But the main thing is our nationwide delivery network. Its flows split roughly fifty-fifty: some orders go to customers’ home addresses, and a comparable share goes to our stores. So the customer chooses a piece of jewellery and can receive and try it on either at home or in one of our shops. And often, if something isn’t right, they choose something similar in design and price, or the same piece in a different size (if it is not in stock in the store, the consultants help place an online order through the app). It is very convenient. About 20–30% of those who order jewellery online and collect it in a store end up buying a different piece. Perhaps they tried things on, compared, and liked something else more, or their expectations were different, they wanted something slightly different… Even though every product card has high-quality video, photos and a description, along with reviews — text, with or without photos, and video — left by customers after their purchases.

– How many users have installed the SUNLIGHT app to date?

– Today the base of active users of our app is about 15 million people.

– How long did it take to achieve that?

– Five years, or more precisely four, although of course the base of loyal customers was built long before the app was created. With every sale we asked the customer for a phone number and email address so we could tell them about new arrivals and promotions.

– A loyal audience loves to express itself creatively.

– Anyone can take part in the various contests held on the SUNLIGHT Play platform. We created this platform in our app not long ago and are now developing it towards a messenger of a new kind, where users will communicate with video content only.

A good website and an ordinary one: what is the difference?

It is a visual story, and you cannot fully grasp it from Tiffany’s website. The thickness of the lines, the composition — this is already contemporary art. Like a Raphael painting, only made by a contemporary artist. It is a new industry where everything is expensive, and people do not yet know that it will soon play the leading role. An ordinary person does not understand how different the masters of the past are from those of today — or how a Russian artist or website maker differs, leaving aside Lebedev and other professionals. People do not see this difference, this gap of fifty to a hundred years. When our tractors still wouldn’t start, their websites already worked well. The difference exists, even if many people do not understand it. And it cannot be explained in words. Lines, composition, background, tones, fonts, how it all fits together and how the piece looks — many different nuances that a programmer found and simply copied. Even how the mouse moves, at what speed, how the picture responds to the cursor — there are masses of nuances here.
