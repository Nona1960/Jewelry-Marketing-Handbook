Glossary

Key terms of modern jewellery marketing — briefly, with links to the chapters that cover them in detail.

AIDA
A model of an advertising message: attention → interest → desire → action. → chapter
AR try-on (virtual try-on)
Trying on jewellery through a phone camera using augmented reality. → chapter
Associative advertising
Advertising through images and associations without a direct call; works better in Europe. → chapter
Average order value
The average amount of a single purchase.
BNPL (buy now, pay later)
Paying in several instalments without a classic loan, e.g. Yandex Split, Dolyami. → chapter
Brand identity
The logo, colours, typefaces and rules by which a brand is recognised. → chapter
Brand portfolio
Several marks owned by one company for different customer segments. → chapter
CAC (customer acquisition cost)
How much advertising and promotion spend it takes to gain one new customer.
Conversion rate
The share of store or website visitors who make a purchase.
CRM
A system for tracking customers and purchase history; the basis for personal offers and mailings.
Data PR
Publishing your own research and statistics, which media pick up as news. → chapter
Directive advertising
Advertising as a call or instruction: “buy”, “give”. Per the chapter 9 table it works well in Russia and the USA. → chapter
Expert content
Guides, articles and reviews signed by a specialist; they build trust and get into AI assistants’ answers. → chapter
GIIS DMDK
Russia’s state system for tracking precious metals and stones; it lets a piece’s path be traced. → chapter
Lab-grown diamond
A diamond created in a laboratory; from 1 Sep 2026 in Russia it is a “faceted synthetic diamond”, weighed in grams. → chapter
Loyalty programme
Bonuses, discounts and gifts that encourage repeat purchases. → chapter
LTV (customer lifetime value)
The profit a customer brings over the whole relationship with the brand.
Marketplace
A platform where many sellers’ goods are sold in one storefront (Wildberries, Ozon). → chapter
Merchandising
Display, grouping and lighting of products that guide the customer’s eye and choice. → chapter
Moissanite
Silicon carbide — a brilliant stone, a popular affordable alternative to diamonds among young buyers. → chapter
Niche
A market segment where a company can lead thanks to its difference. → chapter
Omnichannel
A model where stores, website and app work as one system: a purchase can start in one channel and finish in another. → chapter
Resale / secondary market
Selling pre-owned jewellery; in Russia — pawnshops, buy-back and trade-in. → chapter
Trade-in
Crediting an old piece or scrap gold towards a new purchase. → chapter
UGC (user-generated content)
Photos, videos and reviews made by customers themselves; trusted more than advertising.
