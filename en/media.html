The SUNLIGHT Media Holding

Around the SUNLIGHT jewellery chain sits its own media holding: SUN MAGAZINE for women, DICH’ for men, the industry portal “Jewellery Business” and the WIKI SUNLIGHT jewellery encyclopaedia. The brand does not just sell jewellery — it builds an environment in which people read, argue and learn about it.

SUN MAGAZINE

sunmag.me ↗

A women’s magazine “On beauty and life”: news, horoscopes, values, trends, beauty, columnists, lifestyle, psychology and quizzes. Its “Values” section explains how to wear jewellery, what is in fashion this season and what to give as a gift; articles are tagged #sunlight and sit next to ads for the holding’s brands.

«ДИЧЬ»

dich.media ↗

Media for men, published since 2020: lifestyle, sport, girls, trends, culture, places, personalities and blogs. It runs its own social channels, and the footer of every page invites readers to install the SUNLIGHT app for a gift and 500 bonus points — bringing a male audience to a jewellery store.

«Ювелирное дело»

uvelirnoedelo.ru ↗

An industry portal for people in the jewellery business: encyclopaedia, expertise, business, news, retail, jewellery, education, manufacturing and a blog. It publishes professional material — for example on valuing gemstones — and links to the WIKI SUNLIGHT encyclopaedia.

WIKI SUNLIGHT

sunlight.net/wiki ↗

The “Jewellery Encyclopaedia” information portal: news, precious stones, brands, personalities, “Dronova’s Dictionary”, the jewellery trade, the world of SUNLIGHT, a blog and a library. It carries interviews with Sergey Gribnyakov (e.g. “Corporate values = the new SUNLIGHT”) and holding news such as SUNLIGHT and Academy of Diamonds partnering the premiere of the film “Cosmic Everest”. One click takes the reader to the SUNLIGHT store.

What this case teaches

  1. Content brings customers. A piece on seasonal trends or an interview with the founder works as advertising people choose to read.
  2. Different audiences, different media. A women’s magazine, a men’s outlet, a trade portal and an encyclopaedia each speak to their own readers.
  3. Everything is connected. The media lead to the app, the app to the store, the store to bonuses and the pawnshop: the customer stays inside the SUNLIGHT ecosystem.
  4. Expertise builds trust. “Dronova’s Dictionary” and articles on gemstone valuation show that specialists stand behind the brand.
