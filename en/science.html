The science behind the textbook

Scientific Gemmological Centre

The Scientific Gemmological Centre of the Academy of Diamonds brand is a laboratory for identifying, classifying and valuing gemstones. Its Scientific Director is Professor Nona Dmitrievna Dronova, Doctor of Technical Sciences, Chair of the Collegium of Expert Gemmologists of the Russian Federation.

Part of Academy of Diamonds · Moscow

The Scientific Gemmological Centre (NGC) is part of Academy of Diamonds and works as a gemmological laboratory using modern methods to grade and classify gemstones. It studies rare and valuable stones from around the world, classifies them and passes them to Russian jewellery factories, whose pieces are sold in the Academy of Diamonds stores.

The centre works with major suppliers in Yakutia, Africa, Australia, Colombia, Sri Lanka, India, Brazil and Zambia. Cutters send finished stones to confirm their origin and deposit and to establish precise characteristics — building trust on the world market.

What the centre does

01Classification and identificationOrigin, quality, colour, clarity and other characteristics of gemstones and pearls used in jewellery.
02Passports for jewelleryFinished pieces receive passports confirming the standards, parameters and value of the stones.
03Methodology and teachingGuidelines and textbooks on identifying, classifying and valuing gemstones.
  • Expertise and valuation of stones and jewellery, detecting defects, treatments and synthetic origin.
  • Certification — an official certificate confirming authenticity for sale or insurance.
  • Direct sourcing of gemstones worldwide; mandatory diagnostics of stones shown at trade fairs; staff training.

Research areas

01Diamonds and brilliantsPhysical, optical and technological properties of diamonds; colour change during cutting — where technological mineralogy, mineral physics, optics and applied gemmology meet.
02Gemmological identificationDiagnostic features of natural stones, detection of synthetics and imitations, signs of treatment, optical analysis and inclusions, documentation of results.
03Expert valuation methodologyTheoretical and applied foundations of valuing gemstones and jewellery, treating a piece as an object with mineralogical, technological, artistic and economic characteristics.
04Education and standardsTraining experts and jewellers, curricula and professional development courses, work on professional requirements, certification and industry standards.

Expertise and the professional community

Nona Dmitrievna chairs the Collegium of Expert Gemmologists of the Russian Federation, which works to raise the quality of gemmological research, build the expert community and foster independent, competent and responsible expertise. A key principle is separating established scientific fact, expert interpretation and value judgement — the basis of trust in results and of consumer protection.

She takes part in expert and advisory bodies on technical regulation and standards, professional juries and certification boards, and international events connected with CIBJO, WFDB and EGL.

The “What You Need to Know…” book series was published by the Scientific Centre for Certification and Valuation; the covers carry the seal of the Collegium of Experts in the Certification and Valuation of Jewellery.
