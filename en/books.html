Books by Professor Nona Dronova

Nona Dmitrievna Dronova’s series of books on gemstones, jewellery and the jewellery business — for managers, professionals and buyers. Several were published in partnership with SUNLIGHT and carry the company’s emblems of different years. All books can be read online (in Russian) on the “Science of Stones” platform, and illustrated PDFs can be downloaded free from the Zenodo repository.

For business

Business book · 2008 SUNLIGHT

What You Need to Know About Success in the Jewellery Market. Brilliant Life

Nona D. Dronova

The book this textbook grew out of: its chapters on leadership, staff, competition, advertising and customers became the core of the textbook. The cover carries the 2008 SUNLIGHT emblem.

Keys to power, people management, competitive strategy, the leader’s image, advertising, negotiation. What the head of a jewellery company should be, how to build advertising, work with customers and foreign partners, and how luxury companies succeed.

М.: Научный центр по сертификации и оценке, 2008. — Тираж 4000 экз.
