Author

Sergey Gribnyakov

Sergey Gribnyakov is a jewellery expert, a certified gemstone valuer, founder of his own watch brand SERGEY GRIBNYAKOV and a lover of fine art.

He built Russia’s largest nationwide chain of jewellery stores and the modern jewellery and watch marketplace SUNLIGHT. The SUNLIGHT holding also includes the SUNLIGHT pawnshop network, the Academy of Diamonds and Moscow Chain Factory brands, SERGEY GRIBNYAKOV watches and the “Dating” app.

A path in jewellery

  1. 1996Enrolled at the International Academy of Valuation and Consulting, specialising in the market valuation of gemstones and jewellery
  2. 1997Studied international diamond certification practice at the Antwerp diamond exchange
  3. 2001Postgraduate studies at Moscow State Mining University, Department of Artistic Materials Processing
  4. 2002Co-authored the manuals The Jewellery Business and What a Gemstone Expert Needs to Know with gemmologist N. D. Dronova
  5. 2005–2007Business trips to jewellery centres: Hong Kong, Paris, Vicenza
  6. 2006–2010Took part in international jewellery fairs in Las Vegas, São Paulo and London
  7. 2011–2016Presented SUNLIGHT collections at trade fairs in Moscow and St Petersburg
