Author

Nona Dmitrievna Dronova

Nona Dmitrievna Dronova is a professor of gemmology, Doctor of Technical Sciences and PhD in geology and mineralogy, an educator of long standing with forty years of gemmological practice.

She founded a school of jewellery management in Russia and has written textbooks and reference works on the market for gemstones and jewellery. Her doctoral thesis was devoted to achieving international competitiveness.

  • Chair of the Collegium of Expert Gemmologists of the Russian Federation
  • Chair of the Expert Collegium for Antique Authentication
  • Member of the Union of Artists of Russia since 2002; painter and art theorist
  • Graduate of the jewellery department of the Moscow School of Folk Crafts

In 2002 she co-published, with Sergey Gribnyakov, the manuals The Jewellery Business and What a Gemstone Expert Needs to Know.
