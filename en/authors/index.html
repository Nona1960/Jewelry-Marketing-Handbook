Authors

Nona Dronova and Sergey Gribnyakov

Jewellery Market Marketing was written by two professionals who have devoted all their energy, talent and determination to the jewellery trade: gemmologist and educator Nona Dronova and entrepreneur Sergey Gribnyakov, founder of the SUNLIGHT holding.

Their collaboration began long before SUNLIGHT: in 2002 they co-published the manuals The Jewellery Business and What a Gemstone Expert Needs to Know. The book has an unusual format — both a textbook on jewellery-market marketing and a collection of the authors’ live interviews on topical questions.
